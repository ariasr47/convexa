Verdict

The desk is the design's desk: bare masthead with the controls on the headline row, the structure hero with its ladder and foot, the eight-cell read strip, the wide AI brief beside tape / position / orders, the quiet context band with its filter. Positions, alerts and settings carry the design's compositions — figure cells and the five-card track record, the two-column composer, provider tabs. The scanner carries the score bar, the group header and the three note cards. The light scheme is a token swap everywhere, including the term-structure ribbon that was a black block two days ago.

Composition is byte-identical to the 2026-09-08 cold-verified numbers: the CI-repair commits (contrast tokens, bundled glyphs) moved no region. So this pass confirms that state on the merged tree and adds what a full pass against the design finds beyond heights.

What is not right, most severe first: the desk's cold-load state is still v1's tile grid, not the final shape the design draws and the foundations spec's ship-blocking AC-46 requires. Below that: three clipped-text defects, raw machine timestamps in the AI read, a duplicated field in the analysis drawer, and a cold-start failure that reads "API error 502" where the design writes a sentence.

Findings, most severe first

N1S1

The desk's cold-load state is the retired v1 tile grid, not the final shape.

Before the first bundle the page renders a raised toolbar with a spinner, a title shimmer, and twelve identical tiles in a five-column grid (TickerDashboard.tsx:86-93, mounted at :445-450). The design's Cold load · first paint frame draws the masthead, the field with its ladder and the strip as shimmer — "so the first bundle lands without a jump". The foundations spec makes that binding: AC-46 (ship-blocking) "Cold load shows skeletons in the final shape". QA passed it test-only through the absence-after-load half; the final-shape half was never observed, and phase 2 reshaped the desk without touching the skeleton. On every first load the user watches v1 turn into v2.

Fix. A lane task with a RED test first: the cold-load subtree renders the hero, ladder and strip skeleton shapes; keep the absence-after-load assertion. Re-verify AC-46 by observation.

N2S2

The side column's widget title truncates: "Simulated or…"

In the 379-px side column the Simulated orders widget renders its title as Simulated or… beside the SIMULATED chip and "All orders →" — at 1280 and at 1440. The design's title is Sim orders · SPY. Clipped text is a bug, not a density choice.

Fix. The design's shorter title, or let the title wrap and give the chip and link no fixed reserve.

N3S2

Every Market-context caption is ellipsised.

"As of session Fri Sep 4 · dail…", "At spot $770.19 · 482 strike…", "Today's session cumulative …", "As of session Fri Sep 4 · vs c…". Decided during the 2026-09-08 repair (single line, ellipsis, a title tooltip carrying the full text). It still fails the reader: a tooltip has no tap path, and the caption's job is a three-word basis — the design's are momentum positive, balance tilts calls, flow with the wall, 5d 5.4 · 30d 6.8.

Fix. Author captions of the design's length; remove the ellipsis rule.

N4S2

The ladder truncates "Gamma flip" to "Gamma f…" at 834.

At tablet width the ladder becomes one five-column row and the second label ellipsises; the design's 834 ladder shows all five labels in full.

Fix. Let the label wrap, or drop the per-cell noWrap below ~900 px.

N5S2

Raw machine timestamps and ISO dates in the AI read.

Trade state: As of 2026-08-23T16:00:00.000Z, Expiration 2026-09-18, "implied move for 2026-09-18"; idle state: "Reads the current snapshot, as of 2026-09-08T10:59:51+00:00". The design writes read pinned as of 10:41:52 ET · 48s ago and Sep 18 (15 DTE). The same raw forms sit in the accepted ai-recommendation visual baseline, so the gate pins them.

Fix. One formatter for as-of (ET clock + age) and for expirations, applied in DecisionCard, AiRecPanelBlocks and AnalysisSurface; re-accept the baseline through the receipt flow as a content change.

N6S3

The analysis drawer renders "Time horizon" twice.

AnalysisSurface.tsx:59 lists it in the structured summary and :83 lists it again in the plan fields — visible in the accepted baseline as "Structure · Expiration · Time horizon" followed by "Time horizon · Confidence".

Fix. Drop it from one list.

N7S3

Cold-start failure copy is a raw status.

With the bundle refused the desk shows "⚠ API error 502 · Retry" over the v1 toolbar. The design's frame: "Couldn't load SPY. The structure request failed before any snapshot arrived. Nothing is cached for this desk yet." with Retry, burnt orange plus the icon, the toolbar still usable. Accessibility scenario A11Y-8 asserts the literal "API error 503", so the gate pins the raw copy.

Fix. Change the copy and the assertion together.

N8S3

Live tape figures are boxed cards; the flow spark and captions are absent.

The four tape figures render as bordered, filled cards where the design — and the app's own read strip — divide cells with hairlines. The FLOW spark row is the recorded owner item; the captions ("buyers lifting the ask", "tight · liquid", "mid +0.22% above") are absent — check whether they were cut with cause under canon 24 before adding.

N9S3

The opportunity cell's ring is empty and the tier word is not amber.

App: an amber arc with nothing inside, then "78 ·" and "Actionable" wrapping onto two lines, then "3 setups". Design: 72 inside the ring, Actionable in amber, caption "near wall · IV cheap · 2 setups".

N10S3

Masthead: chips above the title; no day change, no instrument name.

App order is chip row → "SPY · $767.82" → meta. The design leads with "SPY 573.12 +1.84 · +0.32%", then the chips, "SPDR S&P 500 ETF" and the last trade. Neither the day change nor the name is cut with cause in the desk-and-read spec. If the bundle does not carry them, record the cut; if it does, add them.

N11S3

Nav: amber active label, 65 px, e-mail in the bar.

The active link is amber text (TopNav.tsx:182) where the design's active link is phosphor with the amber underline; 65 px against 56; the account e-mail is printed; the session clock chip and bell are absent. Four links · search · ⌘K · palette match.

N12S3

The cap line uses the warning idiom for a non-warning fact.

"⚠ $300 is within your cap of $500." renders with the warning icon in the warning colour — by design (CapAnnotation.tsx: both within and above use the same chrome, AC-60). The canon reserves burnt orange plus the icon for warnings; the design renders the within-cap case as a calm info box.

Owner call. Keep the icon for above, drop it for within.

N13S3

The trade-state AI panel carries an amber outline.

In the produced-read capture the whole panel has a 1-px amber border. The harness performs no interaction there, so it is not the focus ring, and the source was not located in DecisionCard, AiRecPanel or Widget. Rule 16's closed list of non-text amber does not include a panel border.

Needs a source check. If it is a "fresh read" highlight, it is a fourth amber.

N14S3

Elevation differs between the desk and the pages.

Desk widgets carry e1, matching the design's .panel; the settings, positions and alerts page panels render no shadow where the design's are e1.

Latent copy: "TERM STRUCTURE · contango" lowercase vs "Contango"; "OFF-EXCHANGE %" vs "OFF-EXCHANGE"; the term caption prints an 829-day tenor as the far end where the design's window is 7d → 90d; the fixture sentinel "Structure as-of 496527h" (carried). Runtime, not fidelity: the local API's ?dark_pool=true ticker request did not answer within 120 s during pre-market and the plain route stopped answering after it — why the live desk sat on N1's skeleton.

Method and limits

Same harness as the 2026-09-08 audit: the production bundle rebuilt from 3f9d941e and the generated design pages rendered side by side at the design's own widths, measured by DOM census, screenshotted at DPR 1; the self-hosted fonts answered on both sides. Two state captures scripted the same way (cold load with the route held, failure with a 502). 60 harness tests, 0 failures, 1.7 minutes. Every number here is read from the JSON beside the PNGs.

Limits. Authenticated surfaces are fixture-driven; no account was created and no password typed. The live desk could not be walked with today's data because the local API's dark-pool ticker request hung during pre-market. The browser pane's viewport emulation became unreliable mid-session, so the interaction states beyond the palette were not screenshotted from it; their coverage rests on the gates that passed on this tree. The harness's Plex Mono probe is weight-specific and reads false on pages that set Mono at 600 only — a probe artefact, document.fonts.status is loaded on all 58 captures.
