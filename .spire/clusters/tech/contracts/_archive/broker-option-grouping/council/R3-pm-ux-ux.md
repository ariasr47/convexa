## Conflict 1 — fidelity notes inside an expanded group (pm → ux, my §6.6)

> quoted: *"Byte-identical notes across grouped legs render ONCE, at group level, on expand; a leg
> whose note text differs keeps its own."*

**CONCEDED.**

§6.6 is withdrawn in full — I withdrew it myself at R2 as a self-revision, and the pm's conflict
reaches the same place from AC-5 and the BRIEF's "expandable to today's per-leg rows unchanged".
**Member rows keep their own fidelity notes verbatim on expand; nothing is hoisted, nothing is
deduped, nothing is dropped from a leg row.** AC-5 stands as the pm wrote it. Explicitly: I do **not**
want the hoist in v1, so the pm's escalation branch ("it stops being a council question and becomes an
owner question at R3") does **not** fire — no owner ticket is generated by this conflict.

One forward-looking correction, which does not change v1 and does not reopen the concession. The pm's
future-dating trigger — *"if a real book ever produces a group whose expansion carries more than three
identical note lines"* — was calibrated against the pre-correction book ("4 apparent two-leg groups
plus 1 lone NVDA call", now false). On the **corrected** book the largest group is **TSLA at 3 legs**,
so a 3-leg group where each `partial` row carries two notes already produces six note lines, and the
trigger as worded may be met on day one. If the synthesizer writes that trigger verbatim, it reads as
a standing licence to subtract from AC-5 at execution. **Restate the trigger as a condition on the
change, not on a line count:** repeated fidelity notes inside a large expanded group are recorded as a
future-dated read-density item, and any dedup lands as **its own change with its own acceptance
criterion** — additive at group level or a true hoist, decided then — **never as a silent subtraction
from AC-5**.

## Conflict 2 — the leg count on a lone option leg (ux → pm)

> quoted: *"The count is shown on every option holding, grouped or not; the **expander exists only at
> two legs or more**."*

**CONVERGED.**

**I withdraw the placement objection.** The count renders on **every** option holding, lone or
grouped, exactly as the pm ruled. I withdraw arguments (a) and (b) of my raise: (b) is simply wrong —
on the corrected book the tab shows `TSLA 3 · AAPL 2 · META 2 · NVDA 1 · PLTR 1`, and the lone counts
are the two addends that let the owner reconcile 3+2+2+1+1 against the nine option rows in the Webull
app **without expanding anything**. That is a real job, and it is the visible half of the
never-lose-a-position property. (a) survives only as a GATE-Q note, below.

Argument (c) does not survive as a placement objection either, but it is a live **copy** hazard, and
that is the half I keep. A bare `1 option leg` sitting beside `3 option legs` invites both readings
the pm's own §2 forbids — *"this is a single-leg trade"* and *"this leg is unpaired"*. The fix is one
clause, not a missing string.

### Final copy — the lone-leg row (live twice today: NVDA, PLTR)

| Slot | Group header (≥2 legs) | Lone option leg (exactly 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Count line | `3 option legs · grouped by underlying` | **`1 option leg · as Webull reports it`** |
| Expander | chevron inside the identity button | **none, and never a disabled/ghost chevron** |
| Identity cell | `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>` | **plain text — no button, no `aria-expanded`, no `aria-controls`** |
| Qty | (see the crossing note below) | **the leg's own quantity, unchanged from today** |
| Net entry | netted figure + `netted by Convexa` | **the leg's own entry figure with today's label — nothing is netted, so `netted by Convexa` never renders here** |

The clause is plural-safe from the same formatter (`1 option leg` / `N option legs`); the two clause
tails are selected by whether a collapse exists, not by the number.

`as Webull reports it` is doing one specific job: it names the count's **source**, so the `1` is
legible as a fact about the feed rather than as a fact about the trade. It deliberately does **not**
say *why* there is one leg — `not grouped`, `nothing to group` and `the only one on this ticker` were
all considered and rejected, because each states an absence of siblings, which is the "unpaired"
reading the pm banned wearing different words.

**Tooltip on the lone row's count clause** (same affordance the group header's count clause uses;
keyboard-reachable, not hover-only):

> **`Webull reports one option leg on this ticker — and sends no strike, expiry, or call/put with it.
> With one leg there's nothing to group and nothing to net, so this row shows that leg's own figures,
> unchanged.`**

**Layout.** The lone row reserves the chevron's width in column 1 as **empty space** — not a disabled
chevron — so the ticker glyphs align down the column between lone rows and group headers. **Mobile:**
the clause sits directly under the ticker in the same secondary treatment; the card header line is
plain text, because there is nothing to disclose. Both form factors carry the clause, so AC-29's
parity claim covers it.

**Degradation.** The clause is in the **static / snapshot** family, exactly like a group's count:
unchanged on a market-data (SSE) drop, dimmed with the rest of the row at `STALE_SNAPSHOT_SX` on a
stale snapshot.

**Verify by.** The clause's presence and exact wording are visible and intentional → **`review`**
(AC-23's current marking, unchanged, still non-blocking). The negative half is the one that regresses
silently → **`test`**: a 1-leg ticker produces no expander, no `aria-expanded`, no `aria-controls`, no
`netted by Convexa` label and no group treatment of any kind.

**The GATE-Q note that must ride along** (this is all that survives of my argument (a)): the lone rows
were the only entries expected to be byte-for-byte identical to the row the owner live-verified on
2026-08-05, and this ruling adds **exactly one secondary-text clause** to them. Record it as
intentional, or a reviewer will file the added string as a grouping regression on an ungrouped row.
Also worth correcting in passing: AC-1 says "4 collapsed groups + 1 lone leg"; the corrected book is
**3 collapsed groups + 2 lone legs** — still 5 entries.

## Conflict 3 — leg count vs position count (ux → pm)

> quoted: *"With every group collapsed, the sum of the groups' leg counts plus the ungrouped rows
> equals the position count the broker reported. Collapsing never makes a position unreachable or
> uncounted."*

**CONVERGED.**

That sentence bundles a durable invariant with a copy fact that is only true by today's coincidence,
and they separate cleanly:

1. **The invariant (not mine — pm's, and I am not rewording its substance):** nothing is lost to a
   collapse. The sum of the groups' **member positions** plus the ungrouped entries equals the
   position count the broker reported. Stated over member positions, this stays true under any
   adapter. → **`test`**, blocks the ship. The only edit I need is that the phrase **"the groups' leg
   counts" must not survive** in it, because that is the header's rendered string standing in for a
   structural fact.
2. **The copy fact (mine):** the header's visible number is `leg_count` and its noun is `legs`.
   → **`review`**.

### The final count string for a collapsed group

> **`3 option legs · grouped by underlying`**

(plural-safe: `2 option legs`, `3 option legs`; `1 option leg · as Webull reports it` is the lone-row
form from conflict 2, which the group formatter never reaches.)

**Why this stays true when one broker row maps to more than one leg.** The noun names what the number
counts, and nothing else: the number is a count of **legs**, never of rows, holdings or positions. In
the multi-leg-row future the string is still literally true — there really are three legs — and it
stays *checkable* on the shipped grammar rather than by luck, because a member row carrying more than
one leg already has the app's own per-position leg disclosure (`positions/LegDisclosure.tsx`) to
reveal them. The group's disclosure is that same grammar one nesting level up, so `3 option legs`
resolves to three legs by expanding, today in one gesture and tomorrow in two. That is the copy
contract to write down:

> **The header's number and noun must always agree, and the number must equal the legs reachable by
> expanding the group. The header never counts rows, holdings or positions, and the noun `legs` never
> stands in for any of them.**

Concretely: the day `leg_count ≠ position_count` for a group, **the header string does not change** —
it is already right — and the invariant in (1) does not change either, because it was never about
legs. Today both are satisfied by the same book; the split costs the pm nothing now and is the only
thing that keeps the visible count from becoming a quiet lie later. Nothing here asks for a second
number on the row: `N legs in M holdings` is **not** v1 copy and is named only so nobody invents it.

## Not one of the three — a crossing the synthesizer will otherwise hit

Our two R2 self-revisions crossed on the group row's **Qty slot**, so the two R2 files now disagree
without either of us having raised it: the pm's revised AC-16(b) requires *"A group row's quantity slot
renders `—` — never blank"*, while my own self-revision deleted that cell (the identity cell spans grid
columns 1–2, so no Qty cell exists at group level). I am not re-asserting the `—` literal — I withdrew
it and it stays withdrawn. The decidable rule in my lane, which makes either choice safe:

- **Never a blank cell.** Either the identity cell spans column 2 (no cell, no literal, nothing
  renders) **or** a cell exists and `—` occupies it. A rendered-but-empty Qty cell is the only
  forbidden outcome, because it is indistinguishable from an interpolation bug.
- **Mobile (answering the pm's R2 ask):** the card has no Qty column, so the group card **omits the
  quantity slot entirely** — consistent with the spanned-cell form on desktop. This is not the
  `ModeledPl` omission I called a defect: there, one form factor rendered a slot the other dropped;
  here quantity is absent at group level on **both**, so nothing is hidden from one reader that the
  other can see.
