# R1 — ux position: portfolio-demo-platform

## Position

**User and job.** A portfolio visitor should enter a populated Convexa workspace without a password or paid feed, follow a symbol from Scanner to Ticker, inspect sample structure, technical analysis and research, then try a paper position. Rodrigo should find the mode switch and live restoration instructions in the backend atlas. The demo is an example of the real product's interactions, never evidence about today's market.

**Information structure.** Keep the SET Curvature Terminal v2 hierarchy and four top-level links (Scanner · Ticker · Positions · Alerts). Under Ticker, use a symbol-preserving local navigation row: **Overview · Technical analysis · Research**; each view has a direct, lazy-loaded route, a link back to the same symbol, and an entry from the Ticker page. Scanner rows and the command palette can reach the symbol's Overview; the local row makes the new views discoverable without a fifth global link. On a phone, the local row remains keyboard/tap reachable and may be a deliberate horizontal rail; charts reflow rather than overflowing. Do not expose `/_ops/research` as public research. The new surface needs a reviewable design canvas in `docs/backend-atlas/` before build.

**Provenance and vocabulary.** In demo mode, a persistent, text-visible **“Demo · sample data”** banner appears on Landing and throughout the trader shell. Its help text is: **“Prices, positions, analysis and research here are examples. No market feed or brokerage is connected.”** Every time-varying synthetic figure says **“Simulated playback”**, not “live,” “replay,” “session tape,” or “current.” Sample charts and history say **“Sample series”** with the authored scenario date/interval when those fields exist; a fixed scenario is not relabeled with wall-clock `now`. Ticker's existing `live` connection chip, per-figure live dots, pulses and tick flashes are suppressed for synthetic data. A neutral banner is provenance, not a seventh connection state. The existing six-state connection chip remains for actual live mode. This requires a deliberate **DESIGN_SYSTEM.md rule-18 amendment**: its current “one connection chip per page” requirement has no truthful synthetic state. Raise it as an R2 product conflict; do not silently render demo as closed, offline or recorded replay. Verified code: `shared/liveness/liveClaim.ts:47-52` rejects offline/closed/tape, and `sessionTapeStanding.ts:72-81` separates moving display from paid-live standing.

**Where data surfaces.** Seed the existing Scanner rows and palette, Ticker bundle/structure field/read strip/context widgets, visible history and charts, AI read, trade-entry contract choices, simulated orders/Positions, Alerts, personas and settings using one coherent sample scenario. Preserve widget ownership and the canon's figure cells, data basis and not-a-number vocabulary. Optional values can remain absent when the scenario has no defensible basis; say **“unavailable”** in that widget, never zero or a fabricated market fact. A seeded portfolio P/L or mark identifies its sample position/contract and simulated basis. A sample trend/sparkline identifies its **sample series**; an ephemeral buffer cannot be described as observed market history. Off-exchange remains context-only and no-side; sample blocks never imply directional smart money. `TickerDashboard.tsx:926-1182` and `frontend-source-audit.md:29-53` identify the mounted widget families; the intentionally inert add-widget slot is not a missing data panel.

**Demo account and actions.** Show **“Demo visitor”** for the local demo persona and **“Saved in this browser's demo workspace”** near editable demo state. Opening Scanner and Positions needs no credential entry. In demo, replace sign-in/sign-up and Google/Grok connect actions with an explanatory demo state; do not collect email, passwords, API keys, OAuth consent or broker credentials. AI read action copy becomes **“View sample analysis”** and the produced panel says **“Sample analysis · authored for this scenario”**; do not imply a provider responded. The brokerage section says **“Broker connections are unavailable in this demo. Holdings shown here are samples.”** The AI connection section says **“AI connections are unavailable in this demo. No key is needed.”** Trade entry stays the single shared simulated dialog with its normal confirmation; before a write, state **“This changes only your demo book in this browser.”** Broker and live-order affordances remain unavailable. Source checks: `app.tsx:162-170` currently walls Scanner/Positions, `GoogleButton.tsx:46-61` and `AiKeySection.tsx:244-250` can navigate to OAuth, so the demo state must close both paths.

**Technical analysis and Research.** Technical analysis shows sample OHLC chart context, interval, scenario as-of, and named indicator windows/bases (for example “EMA 20/50 · closes,” “RSI 14 · closes,” “ADX 14,” “ATR 14 · price units”) only where the underlying sample series supports them. Its glossary distinguishes RSI momentum, ADX trend *strength* rather than direction, and ATR observed range rather than a forecast. Market Context's digested labels remain its own read; the raw chart series is a separate exploration surface and never silently becomes an AI/scoring input. Research presents an authored **“Sample research note”** with thesis, possible catalysts, risks and the snapshot it references. It does not claim current news, external citations, a live analyst or a generated AI answer. Unsupported or ungrounded sections are withheld instead of padded with invented assertions. Both routes retain symbol context and show the provenance banner immediately.

## Risks I see

- A ticker timer could make synthetic mid/flow look genuinely live, while a session-tape label would falsely claim a recorded market session. Withdraw both claims and show sample provenance.
- Existing account and settings controls can request credentials or navigate to OAuth even when API calls are mocked. The demo surface must close those actions and still allow the public exploration path.
- Fixed fixture expirations, current-time captions, and seeded sparklines can contradict one another or imply a historical record. Every displayed date and series needs the same authored scenario basis.
- Rule 18 of the SET design canon has no truthful connection state for synthetic data. The council needs a product ruling and a narrow amendment before UI implementation.
- The new technical chart can overload the quiet Context band or mislabel model-derived values as observations. Give it its own Ticker view with basis and glossary, and preserve the current Overview hierarchy.

## Assumptions I'm making about the other two lanes

- PM will accept the brief's public, credential-free demo as a separate demo persona, including access to currently auth-walled Scanner and Positions, without reopening live-mode account policy.
- PM will permit Technical analysis and Research as symbol-scoped Ticker views, preserving the four-link top navigation and keeping operator research unlinked.
- Architect will provide one explicit demo provenance signal to all trader surfaces so the UI can suppress paid-live claims and distinguish a simulated playback gap from a real SSE outage.
- Architect will provide a coherent scenario date/interval and source/basis for each displayed sample series and indicator; unsupported values will be explicitly absent rather than defaulting to zero.
- Architect will isolate demo browser writes from live keys and prevent OAuth, paid HTTP/SSE, broker and operator-network fallthrough in demo mode.
- PM will approve a narrow DESIGN_SYSTEM.md amendment for rule 18, since none of its six connection-chip states truthfully names a synthetic scenario.
