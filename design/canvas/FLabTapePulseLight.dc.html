Tape Pulse

Candidate

The mid’s recent trail and the last prints, on a strip with two clocks.

Could land on: the desk’s tape and the mid on the structure field.

Replaying a synthetic tape · nothing here is a live print

How to read it

  • The line is the mid, one point a second over the last five minutes; the dashed line is the structure mid from the last structure snapshot.
  • Prints sit in the lane below: above its line when printed above the mid (▲), under it when printed below (▼). Taller bars are larger prints.
  • Two clocks on one strip, so a fast tape never makes the structure look fresher than its snapshot.

Keys and motion

  • Pause replay holds the strip; Play resumes it. On the demo nothing is a live print: it replays a synthetic tape and says so.
  • New prints fade in over 300 ms; with reduced motion the strip stops scrolling and redraws in place every 5 s.
  • The table lists the last eight prints for keyboards and screen readers.
