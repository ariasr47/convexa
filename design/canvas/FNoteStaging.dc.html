Thesis

The NVDA example pairs a rising close path with visibly larger authored bars to frame a higher-variation scenario.

Evidence

  • The final close is the price anchor for this higher-variation case. Close: 199.17 USD on 2026-10-07. Source: portfolio-2026-10-07-v1:NVDA:2026-10-07
  • The latest bar high-low span supplies its authored range evidence. High 201.11 USD; low 197.23 USD on 2026-10-07. Source: portfolio-2026-10-07-v1:NVDA:2026-10-07

Risks

  • The synthetic range does not estimate real volatility.
  • The example has no semiconductor supply-chain inputs.

Invalidation

Revisit the variation thesis if subsequent authored ranges contract across the same window.
