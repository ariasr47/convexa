Every chart, in every state

The frame owns the states, so no chart can forget one: a loading skeleton at final size, an empty state that teaches, an error with its cause, the last good copy, not live, sample and demo. Shown here on the 2s10s; every primitive renders through the same frame.

Ready and the seven data states

ReadyUpdated Oct 9

The claim, the subtitle, the plot with its event lane and its gap, source and as-of, the table twin.

Loading · final size

Before the first byte. The skeleton is the chart’s final height, so nothing moves when it lands (CLS 0). The title is a skeleton too: the claim is computed from data.

Empty · teaches

The source answered and has nothing yet. Say what would appear and when, never “No data”.

Error · cause + retry

Our request failed. Name the cause and the time, offer Try again; draw nothing rather than a guess.

Stale · last good copy

This cycle failed; the last good copy shows with its marks in textDim and the warning tag says when it was fetched. Never 50% opacity (canon 2026-09-09).

Not live

The data needs a paid feed this site doesn’t run. No value slot, no ghost marks; one link to a labelled sample.

Sample

Inside the live site: dashed frame, hatch stripe, Sample tag, scenario date, a dashed chip that says “shaped like”. Marks draw normally.

Demo

The whole site is the demo: the same treatment, every chip dashed, “Demo · sample data” in place of the connection chip.

Interaction states

Scrubbed

Pointer, touch-drag or keyboard: one hairline, every series ringed, one readout (value first).

Keyboard focus

One tab stop; the authored amber ring on the chart and on the focused point.
