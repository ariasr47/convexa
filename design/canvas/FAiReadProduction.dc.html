What a read sends

Nothing sent

Nothing leaves this site right now: no read runs without the options feed. When one does, it sends exactly this, and the export drawer shows it before you ask:

The computed snapshotlevels, regime, score, implied move
Candidate contractsthe set in the read’s DTE band
Your risk inputsaccount size and per-trade cap, if set
Open simulated positionsthis ticker only, from this browser
The persona’s prompt and a field glossary

Never: another ticker, your name or email, broker credentials, or an API key.

How a read works

  • You ask; it never runs on its own, and it is pinned to the snapshot it read.
  • “No trade” is a complete answer, drawn with the same weight as a trade.
  • A newer snapshot marks the read as older; it never refreshes itself.
  • A read, not an order: a simulated trade needs your review and confirm.
