Chart frameapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/ChartFrame.tsxOne <figure> for every Convexa chart: claim title (a finding), subtitle (metric · unit · period), legend for ≥ 2 series, the plot, source chip + as-of, the table twin. Border is provenance (solid ours / primary, dotted third party, dashed sample). Replaces ChartTitle + FIGURE_SX + per-page captions (publicUi.tsx, Charts.tsx, MarketsPage.tsx).
Axes and gridlinesapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/Plot.tsx · scales.tsSolid hairline grid (line), ≤ 5 y ticks on clean steps, the unit once on the top tick, mono tick labels at 11px; the zero line one step stronger (line2) and named when it means something (“Inverted below 0”). Bars always start at 0; a line may start elsewhere and says so. Replaces dashed grids (Charts.tsx:142-169, MarketsPage.tsx:305-320) and unlabelled units.
Direct labels and collisionsapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/DirectLabels.tsx · labelLayout.tsLines end in their name and last value; a label that would land within 30px of another moves and keeps a leader to its line; a third collision sends the set to the legend + table. Selective labels only: the latest, the extreme, the one that matters. Never a number on every point. Replaces the overlapping right-edge date labels (MarketsPage.tsx:378-399) and label thinning that hides values from sighted readers (Charts.tsx:70-106).
Annotations and event markersapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/EventLane.tsx · events.tsOne lane above the plot for dated events — FOMC decisions, filings (8-K, 10-Q, 10-K), results, CPI — each a labelled chip on a hairline, never a coloured dot alone. Chips within 9% of each other drop to a second lane; under 480px only priority events stay and the rest live in the table. An event is a fact with a source: filings link to EDGAR, FOMC to the Fed. Replaces: nothing (no Convexa chart marks events today).
Range bandapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/RangeBand.tsxAn interval drawn as a chartBand wash with steel2 edge hairlines and labelled bounds — a forecast interval, a year’s range, a valuation range. It states what it is (“3-year 10th–90th percentile”, “95% interval, Wilson”). Never a band that is true by construction (the 2s10s band was its own min–max, MarketsPage.tsx:555-562).
Range barapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/RangeBar.tsxWhere a value sits in its range: a track, the band (when there is one), the median tick, the value marker (phosphor, 4px), and the base diamond — amber, the one amber mark on a chart. Every mark is named under the bar or in the legend line: the median tick was never explained (MarketsPage.tsx:894-926). Zero is marked when the range crosses it (long ↔ short).
Small multiplesapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/SmallMultiples.tsxMore than three series become facets on shared scales, one series each, no categorical palette: three columns, two under 720px of container, one under 480. Used for CFTC categories, curve decomposition and peers. Replaces multi-hue legends that the grayscale test would fail.
Sparklineapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/Sparkline.tsxA sparkline states its scaling (“own range” or “0-based”) and prints its end value; gaps break the line. A sparkbar that can go negative draws below a zero line — the index mini chart drew HOOD’s −90% margin as a 3px stub (ResearchIndexPage.tsx:98).
Crosshair, scrub and readoutapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/Crosshair.tsx · useScrub.tsA hairline snaps to the nearest data x; every series at that x is ringed and listed in one readout (value first, mono; series second; a line key, never a box). Pointer, touch-drag (24px target) and keyboard drive the same index. Hover state lives in a ref + an isolated subtree, so scales never re-render on pointermove (CH7). The readout never holds the only copy of a value: the table twin does.
Keyboard pathapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/useChartKeyboard.tsOne tab stop per chart (role="group", aria-roledescription="chart"). ← → move one point, Shift+← → a month, Home/End first/last, PgUp/PgDn ten percent, Enter pins the readout, Esc clears. The readout text goes to a polite live region. The focus ring is the authored amber ring (rule 6) on the chart and on the focused point — the only amber besides the base diamond.
Data table twinapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/ChartTable.tsxEvery chart carries its numbers: a native <details> “Show the numbers”, a labelled focusable scroll box, a caption, th scope on both axes, mono figures, words for gaps (“not published”, never 0). Closed by default; never the only copy of a value either way.
Reduced motionapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/chartMotion.tsCharts do not animate on load (no grow-in, no count-up). Motion only between related states, ≤ 600ms, transform/opacity, preserving object constancy (the curve morph as the month changes, a bridge step). Under prefers-reduced-motion every one of those is an instant swap with the same end state. None is a keyframe, so the one-motion-moment inventory is unchanged.
Provenance, as-of and unitsapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/ChartFrame.tsxFoot of every chart: the provenance chip (solid primary, dotted third party, dashed sample), then the as-of in mono (“Positions Tue Oct 6 · published Fri Oct 9”). The unit appears once in the subtitle and once on the top tick; the readout and the table repeat it. Mono for a figure Convexa traced; Sans with a dotted rule for an untraced figure (rule 23).
Phone re-layoutapps/dashboard/src/app/shared/charts/Plot.tsxCharts measure their container and re-lay themselves out; they are never scaled down, so text never falls under 11px. Under 480px: short labels, minor ticks and minor events drop, the right gutter narrows, legends move above, small multiples go to one column, columns with long names become horizontal bars. Replaces the 9–10px labels (Charts.tsx:77-78, ScorecardPage.tsx:565-592) and viewBox scale-down under 280px (MarketsPage.tsx:256).
