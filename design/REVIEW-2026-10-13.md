# Design review and v4 redesign trace — 2026-10-13

**Answer:** the design is specific to this product, but its states lie about the hybrid site: it says
"paused" where the truth is "not live without a paid feed", it shows source outages as empty facts, and
the desk collapses to one alert even though most of a ticker's public data is live. v4 adds one
fourteen-state vocabulary, gives every widget an honest state from a source map, and redraws the app
around what is live.

## Top 5 fixes

1. Say "not live" where the paid feed is missing, never "paused" (score 9 · X-01).
2. Show an outage as an outage: "Source down", never an empty list (score 9 · X-02).
3. Keep the desk alive when options data is off: public panels live, options panels explain (score 6 · TKR-01).
4. Give sample data its own unmistakable treatment: dashed, hatched, tagged, dated (score 9 · X-10).
5. Put a page-level provenance legend where the connection chip has nothing to say (score 6 · TKR-04).

## Canon proposals (for the owner; DESIGN_SYSTEM.md is not edited)

- **P-1 · A `not live` state and a hybrid provenance exception to rule 18.** When the server runs without
  the options feed, the connection-chip slot gives way to the page legend "On this page" (counts per
  state), the way demo mode gives it to "Demo · sample data". `paused` keeps its meaning: a feed exists
  and an operator paused it. Notice copy: "Options data isn't live on this site. Dealer structure, flow
  and AI reads need a paid options feed. Rates, positioning, short data, filings and research are live."
- **P-2 · The data-state vocabulary as a closed set**, beside rule 24's: Updated, Delayed by source,
  Computed, Last good copy, Source down, Couldn't load, Not set up, Off, Paused, Not live, Sample, Demo ·
  sample data, Sign in. Each pairs a word with a glyph; failures add burnt orange; none uses green/red.
  The not-live value slot shows `—`, so rule 24 gains no tenth member.
- **P-3 · Sample treatment:** dashed frame + `hatch` stripe + Sample tag + scenario date; no dot, flash,
  trail or clock time. Extends the provenance chip's dashed border to whole panels.
- **P-4 · Build status (live · switch · build · verify · blocked) is design-only**, never product UI.
- **P-5 · Price change is not money moving.** A ticker's day change renders neutral with ▲/▼, like
  research deltas (2026-10-13 row); green/red stay on P&L, flow and gamma sign.
- **P-6 · The current quick symbol is marked by an outline and weight, not amber**, so amber stays
  spent on the one primary action, the active nav and the focus ring.

  Boards show `[EOD PRICE SOURCE]` and status **blocked**.
