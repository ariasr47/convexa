# Charts and data visualization — v5 design pass, 2026-10-13

**Answer:** Convexa's charts are honest in intent (claim titles on Markets, neutral research colour, a
provenance chip) but built one at a time: 52 charts in the app and 30 marks on the boards share no
frame, no axis, no table twin and no keyboard path, so the same defects recur — text under the 11px
floor, no data alternative, silent empty states — and four charts draw something false (a bridge that
cannot go below zero, losses drawn as zero, a missing score drawn as 0, a "5m" trail that holds 1.7 s).
The move is one chart system (`shared/charts/`: ChartFrame, Plot, ChartTable, the keyboard hook), then
every family redrawn on it.

Branch `design/v5-charts` (from `design/v4-app-wide` 4e9e198b) in the worktree
`../worktrees/convexa-design-v4`. Design only: nothing under `apps/`, `.spire/` or `tokens.ts` changes.

## Inventory

**82 entries:** 52 charts and data displays in `apps/dashboard`, 21 further marks drawn only on the
design boards, and 9 planned tool sheets. Overlaps are cross-referenced, not double-counted.

- **Library:** Recharts 3.10.1 is the only chart library (root `package.json:80`). It is used by exactly
  one chart, the operator latency trend. Every other chart is hand-built: DOM, inline SVG, Canvas2D,
  or WebGL2 in the Lab. CH1: one family plus hand-built is acceptable. Hand-built is right for this
  product (§7 CH7: "a prerendered SVG with live text is fine for a static figure"). The finding is the
  missing shared layer, not the library.
- **Text sizing:** research uses `useWidth` (measured width) for three charts. The ticker canvases
  measure `clientWidth` on window resize only. The design's v2 instruments scale fixed SVGs.

## Canon proposals (for the owner; DESIGN_SYSTEM.md is not edited)

Numbered after REVIEW-2026-10-13.md's P-1 to P-7.

- **P-8 · A chart frame's border is its provenance.** Solid for Convexa's own or a primary source,
  dotted for a third party, dashed with a hatch stripe for sample. The provenance chip's grammar
  (Amendments 2026-10-13), applied to the whole figure; extends P-3.
- **P-9 · Charts are neutral and ordinal.** Phosphor carries the claim's series, context steps down
  through steel and a new `chartContext2`; an uncertainty wash `chartBand`. No categorical hues on the
  trader path: past three series, small multiples. The canon calls a data-viz colour scale beyond
  `chartCategorical` an Amendments event, so this needs a ruling. `chartCategorical` stays for ops.
- **P-10 · A claim needs data.** A chart's title states its finding when there is data; loading,
  empty, error and not-live frames name the metric instead; a third party's frame is always named,
  never claimed.
- **P-11 · Sign is never carried by hue alone, gamma included.** Green/red keep their reserved meaning,
  but every use also carries the sign by position (above/below zero) or text (+/−, ▲/▼, "call wall").
  Measured: the pair is ΔE 4.3–4.7 apart under deuteranopia, below the dataviz floor of 6.
- **P-12 · Chart motion between related states is not a motion moment.** A data-driven tween (a curve
  moving to the next month, a bridge step) of ≤ 600ms, transform/opacity, instant under reduced motion,
  with no keyframe. `single-motion-moment.spec.ts` cannot see JS tweens or canvas loops; the class needs
  saying so the frontier pieces do not read as a second orchestrated moment.
- **P-13 · The 11px floor reaches chart text inside SVG and canvas.** The v2 Library note cites 10px
  (`library-page.mjs:121`) and the structure field draws 10px; rule "chips or labels below 11px" should
  name chart labels explicitly.
