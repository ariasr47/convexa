Why it could win

Maximum atmosphere: the field is the whole screen, everything else floats as glass. It is the most cinematic take, the natural home for the WebGL2 twin and the three.js surface, and it makes “liveness” the first thing you feel.

The tradeoff

Density suffers: the seven context reads and setups move behind drawers, and a trader scanning for numbers has to hunt. Floating glass over a moving field also strains legibility and reduced-motion honesty. Better as an optional “Focus” toggle on the built desk than as the default.
