What changes, and why

A name, never a claim

“NVDA daily chart”. Convexa states findings about data it computed; it reads nothing from this frame, so it names it.

A fence with an inset well

Dotted, 8px of well between TradingView’s palette and Convexa’s chrome. Tokens in both schemes (the design source hard-coded a dark rgba).

Loading in words, at final size

“Loading TradingView’s chart…” in the reserved box. The shipped box is empty until the script draws.

A timeout

Blocked after a script error or 12 s without the iframe; the shipped embed waits forever on a stall. The state resets when the symbol changes.

The privacy line names what is sent

Your IP address and this page’s address. The proposal below loads the frame only on request (P-14).

Candles kept

The owner’s ruling: TradingView’s frame shows candles, not a line, in TradingView’s colours.

ScreenA TradingView frame?WhyInstead
Technical tabYes · shippedThe one place a reader comes for price action. The frame is the page’s subject, named and fenced, with Convexa’s own readings marked not live beside it.Keep; add the loading words, the timeout and the reset per symbol.
