What recurs

Each of these is a symptom of SYS-01; the chart system on the next board removes all six at once.

Text under the 11px floor

9–10px in research charts, the structure field and calibration; ~6.6px in the v2 design instruments at 1024

No data alternative

No chart has a table twin; per-strike gamma exists only behind hover on a canvas

No keyboard path

No chart is focusable; tooltips only on pointer

Silent states

Decomposition, 2s10s, the hero field and switched-off panels render nothing when their data is missing

Values drawn as something else

Losses as stubs, absent as 0, gaps bridged, a band true by construction

Colour doing text’s job

Green/red on price change and level moves; an unexplained median tick; a legend hidden on phones
