Touch and hit areas

Every control is ≥ 44px on coarse pointers via one theme rule; desktop density stays 36px. Symbol chips and segmented options grow with it.

No horizontal overflow

Grids use minmax(0, 1fr); long strings ellipsize or wrap; only deliberate rails (read strip, quick symbols, desk strip, the settings tabs) scroll sideways, with hidden scrollbars and a dots hint on the strip.

Type and figures

Display sizes use clamp(); figures never drop below 14px mono; labels stay at the 11px floor; tabular numerals everywhere.

Container queries

Every panel is a container: figure cells drop from 20px to 17px mono under 360px of panel width and to 15px under 280px, whatever the viewport, so a widget reads right in any cell or rail.

Safe areas

The nav pads for the notch; the phone desk pads left and right insets and keeps 76px plus the home-indicator inset free under the docked sheet.

Reduced motion

All liveness is behind prefers-reduced-motion: flashes become a colour hold, sheets snap, the field particles vanish. The hero curve renders drawn.

Both schemes at every size

Dark and light are the same layout — only the tokens change. The desk’s scheme tweak and the light renders on the System page prove it.

Hover has a fallback

Every hover-revealed control (expand, tooltips, the scanner preview) has a tap or focus path; the preview simply hides under 1200px where there is no hover.
