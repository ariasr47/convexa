F2 · A year of the curve, by hand

Markets shows the curve today against a year ago. This lets a reader move through every business day between them, see where the Fed decided, and watch the front end come down while the long end stays: the shape of a year, not two snapshots.

Honest data state

Production

Live: the Treasury’s daily par yield curve, which Markets already reads. A missing day is a gap in the scrubber’s track and says “not published”, never an interpolated curve.

This prototype

Sample, dashed and labelled: a generated year shaped like the Treasury’s, with illustrative FOMC dates.

Canon it needs

P-12 · Chart motion between related states is not a motion moment

Play is a data-driven sequence with no keyframe and no tween; it runs only when asked, stops when hidden, and is replaced by a step under reduced motion. Proposed so it can’t be read as a second orchestrated moment.
