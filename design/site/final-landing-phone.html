Where is price pulled?

Walls, the flip, the magnet — drawn as a field, not a table.

Dealer hedging creates structure you can see: a call wall that caps, a put wall that catches, and a gamma flip where hedging turns from dampening to amplifying. The structure field plots net dealer gamma by strike and drops the live mid onto it, so you read where price is attracted or repelled at a glance.

  • Levels refresh about every minute on the desk and carry their as-of
  • On a ticker desk the mid is tape — it pulses only while the stream is genuinely live.
  • Magnet view shows the same levels as a pull, for the visual thinkers

Is there an edge right now?

A triage score that is honest about what it is.

Proximity to a wall or the flip, IV against realised vol, active setups, a flip transition and optional dark-pool confluence roll into a 0–100 score and a tier. It ranks names for a deeper look. It is not a trade signal, and it never uses the advisory reads.

  • Dormant → Watch → Actionable → Prime
  • Every component of the score is inspectable in the Scanner
  • Context reads (flow, vol, ATR) stay advisory — never score inputs

Read the methodology →

Snapshot, never live

Structure — score, tier, walls, the flip — recomputes on a stated cadence: about a minute on a ticker desk, about three minutes across the scanner. Prices and the mid stream live and are labelled as tape. The two speeds never blur.

Simulated, always

The sim book is paper: no slippage, no partial fills, nothing sent to a broker. A connected brokerage is read-only. Convexa never places an order.

Advisory, never an order

The AI read runs on your own key, pins to a snapshot, and cites what it used. Figures it could not trace render in body type. You confirm every paper trade.
