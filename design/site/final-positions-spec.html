The trader’s simulated book: what is open, what it is worth at its mark, what is waiting to fill, and how past decisions closed. Paper only.

Production and staging

Production · convexa.pages.devThe private-testing wall for visitors: “Sign in to open your positions”, sign-ups closed, a free path to a ticker desk and a link to the demo book. Signed in (the owner), records and user-entered prices work; option marks say Not live.
Staging · the demo accountThe demo account’s seeded book: one open SPY Oct 16 690 C (long 1 at 3.00, trigger fill), one waiting SPY Nov 20 690 C limit 6.50, one closed QQQ Oct 9 630 C (+$48). Marks come from the sample chain and say so: 2.47 at 2:30 PM, −$53.

The three-figure strip stacks; the table becomes one card per position (contract, P&L with sign, basis tags, max loss, days left); orders and track record follow as full-width panels. The wall keeps its copy; its buttons stack at 44px.

Acceptance

  • Production, signed out: the wall reads “Sign in to open your positions”, says sign-ups are closed, and offers Open a ticker desk and “See it on the demo →”. No “Sign up” or “Create an account” anywhere.
  • Staging: the open row reads SPY 690 C · Oct 16, entry 3.00, a Sample mark with its scenario time, and P&L with a − sign as well as red text.
  • Staging: Pending shows the SPY Nov 20 limit 6.50 order; History shows QQQ 630 C closed +$48.
  • Every mark names its basis; no mark cell is ever blank.
  • Reset demo data restores exactly the seed above.
