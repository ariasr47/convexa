The one gate: daily prices licensed for public display

live · TradingView chartblocked · Convexa readings

Settled 2026-10-13: the owner chose TradingView’s free advanced-chart widget for the live Technical tab. It draws daily candles, EMA 20 and 50 and RSI 14 from TradingView’s own licensed, delayed prices, shown as TradingView’s (dotted chip, its attribution untouched, a privacy note).

It is display-only: Convexa reads, stores and computes nothing from it. So every tool in this group that needs Convexa’s own computation (relative strength, realized volatility, correlation, earnings moves, the signal track record) stays blocked until Convexa has a feed licensed to it.
