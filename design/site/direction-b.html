Why it could win

The read is always on screen: scroll through context with the verdict, risk and the “review” action pinned at the right. It mirrors the co-pilot side-panel pattern people already know, and on a wide monitor the desk becomes a two-pane instrument.

The tradeoff

The rail steals ~30% of width from the field at 1440 and forces the read into a tall narrow column, so the two-column brief (why beside risk) is lost. Below 1200px the rail has to collapse to a sheet anyway. The built direction keeps this rail as the phone/narrow form instead.
