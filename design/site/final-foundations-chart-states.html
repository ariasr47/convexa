Every chart, in every state

The frame owns the states, so no chart can forget one: a loading skeleton at final size, an empty state that teaches, an error with its cause, the last good copy, not live, sample and demo. Shown here on the 2s10s; every primitive renders through the same frame.

Ready and the seven data states

ReadyUpdated Oct 9

The claim, the subtitle, the plot with its event lane and its gap, source and as-of, the table twin.

Loading · final size

Before the first byte. The skeleton is the chart’s final height, so nothing moves when it lands (CLS 0). The title names the metric (P-10); the claim waits for data. No tag: P-2 has no word for loading.

Empty · teaches

The source answered and has nothing yet. Say what would appear and when, never “No data”. No tag: the sentence says it.

Error · cause + retry

Our request failed. Name the cause and the time, offer Try again; draw nothing rather than a guess.

Stale · last good copy

This cycle failed; the last good copy shows with its marks in textDim and the warning tag says when it was fetched. Never 50% opacity (canon 2026-09-09).

Not live

The data needs a paid feed this site doesn’t run. No value slot, no ghost marks; one link to a labelled sample.

Sample

Inside the live site: dashed frame, hatch stripe, scenario date, and the sample named once (D1), here by the dashed chip that says “shaped like”. Marks draw normally.

Demo

The whole site is the demo: the same treatment, every chip dashed; the demo bar is the page’s one “Demo · sample data”, and a frame names the sample once, by its chip.

Interaction states · not adopted

Owner NO-13 B (2026-10-25): no shared crosshair and no chart keyboard path. Spec 02 made each chart’s table twin the keyboard path, so these two states stay here as a record, not as a spec. Revisit if scrubbing becomes a goal.

Scrubbed · not adoptedNot adopted

Pointer, touch-drag or keyboard: one hairline, every series ringed, one readout (value first). Not built: no shipped chart scrubs.

Keyboard focus · not adoptedNot adopted

One tab stop; the authored amber ring on the chart and on the focused point. Not built: the table twin is the keyboard path (spec 02).
