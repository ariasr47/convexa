TL;DR · 30 seconds

Revenue more than doubled in a year and the guide implies $412B for FY2027. The price already sits above the base case; what it needs is growth after FY2028, which no filing can show yet.

Part 1

01 · What changed

NVIDIA guided $108B for the third quarter, about 12% above the second. Data Center was $89.0B of the $96.2B, and networking grew faster than compute for the second quarter running.

02 · The case for and against

The case for

▲Supply commitments rose to $279B: demand is booked, not hoped for.
▲Gross margin held at 75% through a product transition.

The case against

▼Free cash flow fell by half as receivables absorbed cash.
▼Equity stakes in customers reached $98.9B, financing some of the demand.
