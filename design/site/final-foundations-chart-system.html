One chart system

Every Convexa chart is built from the same parts: a frame that makes a claim and says where the numbers come from, a plot whose text is drawn at true size, labels that sit on the data, a table twin, one tab stop, and the seven data states at final size. Below: the rules from the canon, the colour roles with their measured contrast and validator verdicts, the mark specs, each primitive in its ready state, and the React module each becomes.

Resolves SYS-01 and the 23 symptoms marked ⟵SYS in design/CHARTS-2026-10-13.md

Six rules, from the canon

A claim, not a label — when there is data

The title states what the chart shows (“The 2s10s stayed positive all year”); the subtitle carries metric, unit and period. Without data (loading, empty, error, not live) the title names the metric: a claim computed from nothing is false. A third party’s frame gets a name too: Convexa makes no claim about data it doesn’t read.

Neutral first

One phosphor series carries the claim; context steps down through steel and chartContext2. No categorical palette on the trader path: past three series, facet into small multiples.

Green and red only when money moves

P&L, flow and gamma sign — and then never alone: the pair measures ΔE 4.3–4.7 under deuteranopia, so position above or below zero and a signed label carry the sign.

Amber is spent

On a chart amber appears twice at most: the focus ring and the base-case diamond. Never a series, never a heat ramp.

The register holds

Mono for a figure Convexa traced (its computation or a primary source); Sans with a dotted rule for an untraced one. Tick labels, readouts and tables follow the figure they show.

The border is the provenance

Solid: ours or a primary source. Dotted: a third party. Dashed with a hatch stripe: sample. The chart frame speaks the provenance chip’s grammar at the size of the whole figure.

Mark specs

Bars

≤ 24px thick, 4px rounded data end, square at the zero baseline; one baseline only; a 2px surface gap between touching bars.

Lines

2px (2.5 for the claim’s series), round joins; gaps break the line and are hatched and named, never bridged.

Points

≥ 8px with a 2px surface ring; hit targets ≥ 24px; labels on the latest, the extremes and the one that matters.

Bands

chartBand wash with steel2 edges; every band says what it is (“trailing-year 10th–90th percentile”, “95% interval, Wilson”).

Grid and zero

Solid hairlines in line, ≤ 5 y ticks on clean steps, the unit once on the top tick; zero in line2 and named when it means something.

Text

HTML at true size, 11px floor, mono ticks; under 480px of container short labels, fewer ticks and a narrower gutter: re-laid out, never scaled.

Chart frameChartFrame (new)

One <figure> for every Convexa chart: claim title (a finding), subtitle (metric · unit · period), legend for ≥ 2 series, the plot, source chip + as-of, the table twin. Border is provenance (solid ours / primary, dotted third party, dashed sample). Replaces ChartTitle + FIGURE_SX + per-page captions (publicUi.tsx, Charts.tsx, MarketsPage.tsx).

Axes and gridlinesPlot · YAxis · XAxis (new)

Solid hairline grid (line), ≤ 5 y ticks on clean steps, the unit once on the top tick, mono tick labels at 11px; the zero line one step stronger (line2) and named when it means something (“Inverted below 0”). Bars always start at 0; a line may start elsewhere and says so. Replaces dashed grids (Charts.tsx:142-169, MarketsPage.tsx:305-320) and unlabelled units.

Direct labels and collisionsDirectLabels + labelLayout.ts (new)

Lines end in their name and last value; a label that would land within 30px of another moves and keeps a leader to its line; a third collision sends the set to the legend + table. Selective labels only: the latest, the extreme, the one that matters. Never a number on every point. Replaces the overlapping right-edge date labels (MarketsPage.tsx:378-399) and label thinning that hides values from sighted readers (Charts.tsx:70-106).

Annotations and event markersEventLane + events.ts (new)

One lane above the plot for dated events — FOMC decisions, filings (8-K, 10-Q, 10-K), results, CPI — each a labelled chip on a hairline, never a coloured dot alone. Chips within 9% of each other drop to a second lane; under 480px only priority events stay and the rest live in the table. An event is a fact with a source: filings link to EDGAR, FOMC to the Fed. Replaces: nothing (no Convexa chart marks events today).

Range bandRangeBand (new)

An interval drawn as a chartBand wash with steel2 edge hairlines and labelled bounds — a forecast interval, a year’s range, a valuation range. It states what it is (“3-year 10th–90th percentile”, “95% interval, Wilson”). Never a band that is true by construction (the 2s10s band was its own min–max, MarketsPage.tsx:555-562).

Range barRangeBar (new)

Where a value sits in its range: a track, the band (when there is one), the median tick, the value marker (phosphor, 4px), and the base diamond — amber, the one amber mark on a chart. Every mark is named under the bar or in the legend line: the median tick was never explained (MarketsPage.tsx:894-926). Zero is marked when the range crosses it (long ↔ short).

Small multiplesSmallMultiples (new)

More than three series become facets on shared scales, one series each, no categorical palette: three columns, two under 720px of container, one under 480. Used for CFTC categories, curve decomposition and peers. Replaces multi-hue legends that the grayscale test would fail.

SparklineSparkline (new; replaces lib sparkline + MiniChart bars)

A sparkline states its scaling (“own range” or “0-based”) and prints its end value; gaps break the line. A sparkbar that can go negative draws below a zero line — the index mini chart drew HOOD’s −90% margin as a 3px stub (ResearchIndexPage.tsx:98).

Crosshair, scrub and readoutCrosshair + useScrub (new)

A hairline snaps to the nearest data x; every series at that x is ringed and listed in one readout (value first, mono; series second; a line key, never a box). Pointer, touch-drag (24px target) and keyboard drive the same index. Hover state lives in a ref + an isolated subtree, so scales never re-render on pointermove (CH7). The readout never holds the only copy of a value: the table twin does.

shared/charts/Crosshair.tsx · useScrub.ts · 8 states on the states board and in the site library

Keyboard pathuseChartKeyboard (new)

One tab stop per chart (role="group", aria-roledescription="chart"). ← → move one point, Shift+← → a month, Home/End first/last, PgUp/PgDn ten percent, Enter pins the readout, Esc clears. The readout text goes to a polite live region. The focus ring is the authored amber ring (rule 6) on the chart and on the focused point — the only amber besides the base diamond.

Every chart carries its numbers: a native <details> “Show the numbers”, a labelled focusable scroll box, a caption, th scope on both axes, mono figures, words for gaps (“not published”, never 0). Closed by default; never the only copy of a value either way.

Reduced motion— (CSS + useReducedMotion)

Charts do not animate on load (no grow-in, no count-up). Motion only between related states, ≤ 600ms, transform/opacity, preserving object constancy (the curve morph as the month changes, a bridge step). Under prefers-reduced-motion every one of those is an instant swap with the same end state. None is a keyframe, so the one-motion-moment inventory is unchanged.

Full motion

Curve points tween 400ms ease-out from Sep to Oct; labels cross-fade 160ms. Interruptible: a new month retargets from the current position.

Reduced motion

The October curve replaces September in one frame. The month label and the readout change in the same frame. Nothing else differs.

Phone re-layoutPlot container queries (useWidth / ResizeObserver)

Charts measure their container and re-lay themselves out; they are never scaled down, so text never falls under 11px. Under 480px: short labels, minor ticks and minor events drop, the right gutter narrows, legends move above, small multiples go to one column, columns with long names become horizontal bars. Replaces the 9–10px labels (Charts.tsx:77-78, ScorecardPage.tsx:565-592) and viewBox scale-down under 280px (MarketsPage.tsx:256).

Third-party frameThirdPartyFrame (new; wraps TradingViewEmbed)

A frame Convexa does not draw: a descriptive name, never a claim (Convexa makes no claim about data it doesn’t read); the dotted chip above it; a dotted fence with an 8px inset so the party’s own palette never touches Convexa’s chrome; the party’s attribution untouched below; the privacy line. No crosshair, no table twin, no state words of ours inside it. Replaces the literal rgba fence in TradingViewEmbed (dark-scheme value used in both schemes).
