02 · Four memoriesWhat we currently store

Capture is a one-way leaf: score, fingerprint, and the trader SSE never import it. A failed write logs and moves on. Public site 404s /api/_research/*.

Layer A is the thing the Options subscription buys. Layer B is a handful of numbers derived from it. We currently rank B.

Layer A — the paid options book

Each snapshot is a frozen VendorBundle: the full chain (strike, right, expiry, OI, IV, greeks, volume, optional NBBO), plus daily bars, RTH minutes, and trade prints, keyed (symbol, as_of) with nanosecond RFC3339 identity. Source is live_massive or fixture. Secrets are stripped. Append-only: a second cycle does not overwrite the first.

Written off the trader path from scanner / ticker cold compute (load_or_compute / cache-miss serve). The warmer (refresh_analytics) is excluded on purpose — it would stampede the same book.

Retention is dual, and the count cap is winning. Defaults: CHAIN_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_SECS=1209600 (14 days) and CHAIN_SNAPSHOT_MAX_PER_SYMBOL=48. Both apply. On this box the 48-slot ring filled in ~17 hours of 19 Aug. 2,993 of 3,011 entries are that one calendar day. Fourteen-day age prune never gets a chance to matter.

What a paid book contains (and what it does not)

Inside each snapshot

  • Every option contract on the snapshot: strike, call/put, expiry, OI, IV, greeks, volume.
  • Synchronized spot + display spot + market phase.
  • Daily bars (for HV) · minute bars (VWAP) · prints (dark-pool / blocks, context only).
  • Provenance: live_massive vs fixture, frozen as_of.

Not bought, not stored

  • Massive point-in-time historical options chains (separate product — P7).
  • Historical IV surface as a vendor series (we keep our own ATM IV diary).
  • Index underlyings we do not scan (VIX/SPX) unless they are in the universe.
  • Overnight session (Massive is 4 AM–8 PM ET). Unsolved; not a capture bug.

03 · Four toolsWhat we currently back-test

“Back-test” here does not mean a broker P&L simulator. It means: replay our compute on a frozen input, then (optionally) join a forward stock-bar label. No tool writes live score, tier, or fingerprint.

score-lab never reloads a chain. batch-backtest never attaches a next-day label. The join is the missing meeting point — shipped yesterday, not yet run on a multi-week corpus.

P0 join-outcomes

Attaches 1d / 5d session-count labels from Massive daily stock bars to digested samples. Writes structure_outcomes. Zero Options calls.

cargo run -p api-rust -- join-outcomes --symbols SPY,QQQ,IWM

P2 score-lab

Ranks five candidate signals against three labels, pooled and per-symbol. Comparative report is the default. Strict read: a degraded column aborts the run rather than faking “absent.”

cargo run -p api-rust -- score-lab --outcomes data/research/outcomes_….json --horizon 1d

P3 batch-backtest

Reloads stored books, runs Path-B compute_analytics_from_vendor, emits score / tier / walls / flip / display_spot. Zero Massive. Time split flags exist; a hollow split is refused.

cargo run -p api-rust -- batch-backtest --symbols SPY,IWM --from … --to …

JOIN replay-outcome-join

Pairs replayed scores with 1d/5d labels. p0 is capture-time display_spot, never a silent session close. Honest states: no snapshot ≠ empty book ≠ no label ≠ no_p0. Stocks fill only if labels are missing.

cargo run -p api-rust -- replay-outcome-join --replay data/research/batch_….json

The only published slice (11 Aug, 221 rows, same afternoon). Composite vs large move: AUC 0.467, top-decile lift 0.17 — worse than chance. Composite vs structure_event: AUC 0.932 — mostly definitional (score is built from wall/flip proximity). Pass bar is multi-week, operator-judged, never a per-run “AT BAR.” That slice is too thin to retune anything.

What we are not back-testing yet

  • The live score on the exact paid chain vs next-day move — tool exists; corpus is one session and missing SPY/QQQ.
  • AI recommendation quality (P4). Blocked until P2–P3 are boringly used.
  • Options P&L / fill paths / broker outcomes. Labels are stock-bar session returns, not contract P&L.
  • Intraday 30-minute horizons. Still 1d / 5d only.
  • Vendor historical chains before we existed (P7). Extra Massive product. Do not buy to paper over the cap.

04 · The real holeTools shipped. Calendar has not.

We can measure

  • Whether digested score ranked next-day |move| on 10 Aug (it did not, on that slice).
  • Whether geometry candidates differ from the composite (comparative lab).
  • Whether Path-B replay of a stored book is byte-repeatable (P3 tests).

We cannot honestly claim yet

  • The score “works” or “doesn’t” on paid books — one session, no 5d labels.
  • A train/validate split — hollow split is correctly refused.
  • SPY/QQQ paid-book performance — those files are missing or fixture-only here.
SPY and QQQ are the names the lab already uses — and they are the ones without paid books on this box. SPY’s file is the 1970 GATE Q fixture. QQQ has no file. IWM has 25 live books (18–19 Aug). Capture follows scanner cache-miss / ticker cold compute, not “always keep the liquid names.” If those names stay warm in gex-cache, the paid chain is never appended.

05 · Keep what we pay forDo not buy more Massive. Stop deleting today.

Stocks $199 already labels the next session. Options $199 already returns the book. Maturity is a retention design, not a new vendor SKU.

One full chain per name per session is enough to ask “did yesterday’s book rank today’s move?” That is the whole product question.

Keep (no new spend)

  • Session-level archive that prune cannot touch (object store or dated folder).
  • Force-capture SPY / QQQ / IWM at least once per RTH even if gex-cache is warm.
  • Stocks aggs join for labels — already paid, already built.
  • CHAIN_SNAPSHOT_ENABLED=true. Turning it off to save disk is how we go back to throwing the invoice away.

Do not (yet)

  • P7 Buy Massive hist-chain / hist IV / indices to backfill. Capture what we already fetch first.
  • Raise the 48 cap and keep full-size intraday books for 14 days. That is terabytes (14 × 48 × 70 × ~50 MB).
  • Put unbounded BYTEA chains on the Railway Postgres volume as the long-term archive.
  • Redistribute stored snapshots on a trader route. License: our research, never a feed.

07 · SequenceRecommendations, in order

One job at a time. Later steps are cheaper if the first two land.

1

Thin capture to one session book

After each RTH (or on the last slow-lane of the day), copy one snapshot per symbol into an archive prune cannot delete. Leave the 48-slot ring as an optional same-day buffer. This single change turns $398/mo into a dated corpus instead of a 17-hour loop.

2

Guarantee the names we already measure

SPY, QQQ, IWM must produce a session book even when gex-cache is warm. Today the lab’s own tickers are the ones missing paid chains. That is an operational bug, not a vendor gap.

3

Smoke the new join on what we have — honestly

Run batch-backtest then replay-outcome-join on IWM (25 live books) or a 19 Aug subset. Expect 5d = not-computable. Paste the tool’s evidence block. Do not retune. The point is to prove the pipe on real files, not to harvest an AUC.

4

Keep the cheap weekly digest ritual

The structure diary already spans nine days. Continue join-outcomes → score-lab --horizon 1d on SPY/QQQ/IWM. Paste, don’t retype. This is the only series that can grow 5d labels in the next two weeks.

5

Wait for four to six session weeks of archived books

Then a time split is real, 5d labels exist, and a paid-book AUC means something. That is the first moment a score-changing feature could even be proposed — still under score-change-is-deliberate-and-measured, with before/after on real tickers and a fingerprint-basis bump.

6

Only then: AI lab (P4), then shadow (P5)

Frozen export packs, multi-model, cost/latency. Champion policy. No silent swap. Product replay UI (P6) stays internal and badged. Hist-chain (P7) remains “only if P2–P3 are boringly solid.”

7

Prod storage: not unbounded BYTEA on the app database

Local JSON is already 3.8 GB for one day of unthinned books. Session archive belongs in object storage (or a dedicated volume) with identity (symbol, et_date). Postgres can keep metadata + labels. The chain body is the expensive part — keep it, just not in the OLTP row.

Decision impact, if we do 1–5. You will be able to say, with a pasted report: “on the exact Options book we paid for that session, did the score rank the next session’s large move — and over how many weeks?” That is the question the 11 Aug digest slice asked and could not settle. Buying hist-chain does not answer it. Keeping Tuesday’s book until next month does.

Standing rules this plan must not break

  • Authorizes: nothing — a report never ships a live weight.
  • Additive keeps score byte-identical until a deliberate, measured score feature.
  • Best-effort capture — a store failure never takes down bundle / scan / SSE.
  • Operator vs trader path — research CLIs and /_research stay unlinked from the desk.
  • Absent is not none — no snapshot, empty book, no label, no_p0 stay distinct.
  • Dark-pool is context only — prints in the book are not a scored signal.
