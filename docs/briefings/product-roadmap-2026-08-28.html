04 · The time machineDay replay is a new product, not a flag on batch-backtest

You asked: if we stop paying Massive, play back a collected day so the frontend updates from open to close as if it were live. That is the right insurance policy. The fixture port we have today cannot do it.

Layer 1 — the tape (price)

Every SSE tick we already show (mid, spread, last, net flow) written append-only for a closed watchlist (start with SPY/QQQ/IWM). Or, next morning, the entitled flat file. This is what makes the header and live tiles move.

Layer 2 — the structure (GEX)

Timed Path-B snapshots through the session (not only first-wins at midnight, not only 48 reprints that prune). This is what makes walls, flip, score, and AI inputs walk. Vendor cannot reconstruct this after the fact.

Do not pretend a midnight book is a day. QQQ 24 Aug in the audit is a valid first-wins identity with 11,510 contracts and zero trades. Replaying it would freeze the open. Capture cadence for replay is a product decision: e.g. one structure snapshot every 5–15 minutes on the watchlist, plus the full tick tape.
1
Tape it while paying. Bounded watchlist. Provenance live_massive. Fail loud if the day is incomplete.
2
Download the entitled flat file the next morning as a second, labeled artifact (tape reconstruction), never as a fake live book.
3
Mock adapters implement the existing ports. A replay command points the server at a (symbol, et_date). The ticker page does not know it is not Tuesday.
4
Then you can cancel Massive and still demonstrate, debug, and study days you actually lived through. You cannot mint new days.

05 · Math and modelsOpportunity score, “AI everywhere,” and why RL waits

What the score is (and is not)

A 0–100 triage heuristic: proximity to dealer levels, IV extremity, stacked setups, a transition bonus. Higher means “more is lining up near a tradeable level,” not “price will trend.” The 10 Aug bins say the highest scores behaved like pin risk, not like a breakout alarm.

Until weekly labs on a multi-week, per-symbol corpus say otherwise, treat Prime as “pay attention,” not “size up.”

Do this with the score
  • Keep the weekly ritual. Paste the tool’s own evidence block. Never retype numbers.
  • Freeze score-lab-b0b-outcomes when MASSIVE_API_KEY is in the build env. Never synthesize labels.
  • Add vol-norm onto lab rows as a candidate, not as a live weight.
  • Wait until ~23 Sep–7 Oct (4–6 weeks from 26 Aug) before any time-split story.
Do not do this with the score
  • Change weights from one afternoon of 221 rows.
  • Alert harder on 80+ until the pin pattern is gone in a bigger sample.
  • Train a neural net or RL agent on the diary. Sample size is a rounding error.
  • Let the AI invent a second score. It already fabricated 44% of asserted numbers before grounding shipped.

AI — what actually works in 2026

Hedge funds (AIMA 2024: 95% using gen AI) put models on filings, transcripts, and copilot research with citations. Bridgewater’s public “Pocket Analyst” pitch is hours of exploratory research in minutes, not a buy button. Man Group’s AlphaGPT generates and tests signals. TradeRank’s live LLM paper-trading season: 0 of 14 models beating Bitcoin as of this week. The model-as-trader demo is content. It is not your edge.

Convexa already has the hard part most “AI trading” apps skip: a computed bundle, a closed vocabulary for missing fields, and a rule that a number the model asserts must trace to something we calculated. “AI everywhere” without that is how you get another 44%.

Squeeze, in one paragraph so we can stop re-deriving it

Academic work in 2025 finds short squeezes are real, rare, and short-lived (about 15% of activist short attacks; quarterly, ~10% of names see a “market squeeze” day). A 2019–2023 gamma-squeeze identification paper finds 669 candidate events with a ~5% average abnormal return over the following month — a selected sample, not a scanner you can run every morning. OptionWalls, which actually publishes receipts, shows a squeeze score ≥70 with 51.7% hit rate and +0.15% median 5-day underlying return (n ≈ 5.8k). That is not a business. It is a lottery ticket with honest marketing. Convexa should keep saying when price is near a call wall with rising call OI — that is already on the chain we pay for — and refuse to brand it “squeeze.”
