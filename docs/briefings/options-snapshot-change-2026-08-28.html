Measured on disk No Massive calls Authorizes: nothing

The options snapshot does not replace itself every minute. Open interest sleeps. Greeks twitch because the stock moved.

Convexa · 28 Aug 2026 · compared stored chain snapshots + scanner structure diary · local data/

A new full chain every 60 seconds is mostly yesterday’s dealer book, repriced for today’s spot. Walls change on a tens-of-minutes clock. The flip ticks with price. Capture that way, not “every options tick.”

This is a measurement of books we already paid for and kept, not a guess and not a score change. Two stores: the raw Massive chain (data/chain_snapshots) and the GEX map the app actually shows (data/scan.json history).

Why the flip and the score twitch. Opportunity score pays up to 40 of 100 points for sitting near a wall. The analytic flip is a function of spot versus that wall. If the wall holds and the tape walks $2, both the flip and the score “change” without the dealer book having been rewritten. That is layer 4, not layer 1.

Median |Δ net GEX| on those 6-minute RTH samples: 2.6% (mean 8.6% — a few messy pairs pull the average).

07 · So what do we keep?Match the clock of each layer

LayerKeepCadence that matches the measurement
Strikes + OI One prune-proof session book + the OCC update when it lands Daily. Premarket in this sample.
Vendor greeks Full snapshots (we do not recompute greeks) 5–15 min for walls. 60s only if you want the live-poll movie. Gzip; don’t diff.
Stock tape SSE ticks (or next-morning stock trades file, filtered) That is what makes price tiles move. Tens of MB / name / day.
Watchlist SPY / QQQ / IWM + names you trade Not 69 names at 60s. Scanner diary already covers breadth cheaply.
Bad books Drop them Contract-count collapse like 13,935 → 9,050 is not structure.
3 names × 15 min × 30 days stays in the low tens of gigabytes. The same at 60s starts to want the remote body store. RAM for replay stays small: current snapshot + a tick buffer. Stream the rest like a video.

08 · Honest edgesWhat this test is not

  • Local ring, not the 26 Aug production session books. Densest chain day here is 20 Aug.
  • SPY/QQQ/IWM are thin in the ring because a warm GEX cache skips capture. History in scan.json is the better clock for walls.
  • Flip “changed” is almost always a small analytic move, not a new dealer structure. Walls are the sticky object.
  • A naive sum of 13k gammas can explode when net GEX crosses zero or a truncated chain appears. Trust Path-B net_gex in the diary.
  • Nothing here retunes opportunity_score.
Bottom line for replay. Tape the stock. Snapshot the chain on a walls clock (5–15 min), or 60s if you want the live feel. Treat OI as daily. Do not buy “every options tick.” That is the day you can play back with Massive unplugged.
