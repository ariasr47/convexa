# Convexa Research View, archived 2026-10-28

The "Convexa Research View" design canvas
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/HxZArSCsbrjQCx8VBbYRw7>) as it stood before it became a pointer to
"Convexa Desk Redesign" (<https://claude.ai/artifact/3ooV24ayHwh52t3qAhFiBe>). Its 11 boards were
last changed on 2026-10-13. The copy here is from the artifact's version `1793230716-6e2f`.

- **Research view:** the note view on a desktop (the CBRS note) and on a phone, the research index,
  authoring (Markdown to visuals), and freshness and updates.
- **v3 · Public data:** Markets, ticker research (NVDA), Filings, Scorecard, Markets on a phone, and
  the v3 kit.

The ruled versions of these screens are on the Research page of Convexa Desk Redesign. Nothing in
the repository generates these boards, so the canvas's own `project/` files are kept here, byte for
byte. They need the Design canvas's runtime (`support.js`) to render, so a browser opening one
directly shows unstyled markup. The artifact's version history also keeps them.
