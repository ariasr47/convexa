# The verdict

## What the price implies | The price pays for better economics, not just more growth.

```implied
```

The block solves the base case for the yearly growth that would justify the price. Read it with care: at base inputs, a new dollar of revenue earns about its cost of capital (calc: 20% margin × (1 − 21% tax) × $0.8 of revenue per $1 invested ≈ 12.6%, against an 11.5% cost of capital). Extra growth then adds little value, so the solved rate runs far past any base rate: growth above 45% a year happened in only 1.3% of five-year windows among large companies [29].

What closes the gap is margin and capital per dollar of revenue together. The sensitivity grid in Valuation shows both.

```variant
Backlog conversion | Most of the $25.4B becomes revenue on schedule: 22% by mid-2028, 43% in the two years after [1]. | | Q3 and Q4 core revenue against $214–216M and the ~$269M implied for Q4 (calc) [2]; RPO in each 10-Q.
Mature operating margin | Close to the semiconductor average (35% [26]) on a business that is now 61% cloud (calc) [2]. | | Core operating margin once the 2027 ramp ends; the first FY2027 guide.
Capital per dollar of revenue | Closer to a chip vendor's than to a GPU cloud's [27][28]. | | Capex against revenue growth in each 10-Q; H1 2026 capex was 1.37× core revenue (calc) [1][2].
OpenAI | The 750MW commitment runs its course and the 1.25GW option gets used [1]. | | Option exercise, or a termination, reduction or loan acceleration in a filing.
```

## Earnings quality | GAAP revenue fell in Q2 because the value of customer warrants is booked against it.

```bridge "Q2 2026: core revenue to GAAP revenue, $M"
Core revenue | 209.9
Customer-warrant amortization | -44.3
Pass-through revenue | 14.5
GAAP revenue | 180.1
```

- **The warrant charge is non-cash.** Customers paid in full; the charge is the value of the equity Cerebras gave them, spread over the revenue it earns. $1.13B of customer-warrant assets is still to be charged against future revenue [1]. This note counts the warrants as shares instead (see Valuation), so the dilution is counted once.
- **"Core" also leaves out stock pay.** Q2 stock pay was $377.0M, mostly awards that vested at the IPO, which took the GAAP operating margin to −265% against −16% core [2]. The valuation's margins include stock pay for future grants.
- **2025's profit wasn't operating profit.** GAAP net income of $237.8M sat on a $145.9M operating loss [4].

# The debate

## Case for and against | The bull case is contracted demand; the bear case is that the contract belongs to one customer.

```case
+ The backlog dwarfs revenue | $25.4B contracted against an $885M year; about $5.6B is due by mid-2028 (calc) [1][2]
+ The mix is shifting to cloud | Core cloud revenue rose 287% to $127.7M, 61% of core revenue (calc) [2]
+ Outsiders vouch for the speed | AWS chose Cerebras for decode on Bedrock and took a warrant; AMD co-develops; OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 Sol at 750 tokens/s on it [1][2]
+ It is funded and skips GPU bottlenecks | No HBM or advanced packaging; $8.6B of liquidity and an undrawn $850M revolver [1][2]
- The backlog is one customer's commitment | "A significant amount" of RPO is OpenAI's, and OpenAI also buys from AMD, Broadcom and Nvidia [1][21]
- Core flatters the economics | GAAP gross margin was 14.2% against 40.6% core, with $1.13B of warrant charges still to come [1][2]
- It is turning into a capital-heavy cloud | H1 capex of $549M exceeded H1 core revenue, and the Q3 guide deepens the operating loss [1][2]
- The price needs the bull case | At base inputs the model's value is a fraction of the price (Valuation)
```

## Pre-mortem | If the thesis fails, the likeliest story starts with OpenAI moving work elsewhere.

```premortem
It is late 2027. OpenAI moved its fastest tiers to Nvidia and its own Broadcom chips, trimmed the 750MW plan, and the loan came due. | #3
Data centers came online two quarters late; Q3 2026 missed $214M and 2027 revenue doubled instead of tripling. | #1
Rented capacity and new leases squeezed the cloud; core gross margin slid below 38% and stayed there. | #2
A shrinking RPO showed customers cutting commitments, not just consuming them. | #4
The build cost more than planned, and Cerebras sold new shares in 2027. | #5
```

The opposite pass: the view that the price is too high fails if OpenAI uses its 1.25GW option in 2027 and AWS puts Cerebras inside its own data centers, which would lift margins and capital efficiency together.
