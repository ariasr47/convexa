Tesla makes electric cars and grid batteries, and is spending heavily to turn its cars into robotaxis and to build humanoid robots. At $378 the company is worth about 300 times last year's operating profit, so the price assumes autonomy and robots become a far larger and more profitable business than cars have ever been. Whether robotaxi becomes a large, high-margin network decides it; today it is a small fleet in six metros.

# The verdict

## What the price implies | The price needs autonomy and robots to dwarf the car business, at a growth rate almost no large company has reached.

```implied
```

The base case below lets the business outside autonomy recover to its five-year average margin, keeps energy growing and gives robotaxi and FSD about a tenth of revenue by 2033. To reach today's price with those margins, revenue would have to grow at a rate no company with more than $50B of sales has sustained for five years. From 1950 to 2015, five-year real growth above 20% a year happened in 1.0% of such windows (calc), and the median was 1.5% [32].

Today's profit, capitalized at the cost of capital, covers only a sliver of the price. Almost all of it rests on businesses that are not yet in the accounts: unsupervised robotaxi, the Cybercab, Optimus.

