SpaceX is now three businesses in one filing: rockets, Starlink broadband, and, since the February 2026 xAI merger, an AI cloud with X and Cursor attached. At $2.3 trillion diluted, the price assumes revenue climbs from a $31B annual run-rate toward the scale of Musk's $1 trillion goal. What decides it is whether the AI compute it buys at about $18B a quarter keeps earning more than it costs once today's scarcity pricing fades.

## What the price implies | The price pays for a company far larger than this note's bull case reaches.

The block solves the base case for the yearly growth in 2028–2031 that would justify the price, with 2027 revenue held at $100B. At base inputs a new dollar of revenue earns more than its cost of capital (calc: 22% margin × (1 − 21% tax) × $0.9 of revenue per $1 invested ≈ 15.6%, against a 10.5% cost of capital), so faster growth does add value. The rate it needs still sits far outside the record: among large companies, growth above 45% a year happened in only 1.3% of five-year windows, and the median three-year rate was 5.4% [39].

Today's operating profit supports none of the price: the company lost $143M from operations in Q2 [2], so the small share the block shows is net cash. Everything rests on the AI build and Starlink compounding together.

- **Tenants.** Anthropic pays $1.25B a month for Colossus and Colossus II compute in Memphis through May 2029, and either side can end it on 90 days' notice, per the S-1 as Reuters reported [22]. Musk later called it a 180-day lease at SpaceX's request [22]. Google pays $920M a month from October through June 2029, with a Sep 30 hardware deadline; either side can end it on 90 days' notice after Dec 31, 2026, per a June 5 filing as summarized in the press [23][42].
- **Concentration.** Customer A was 18.3% of Q2 revenue across all three segments; Customer B was 19.5%, all in AI [7]. Neither is named; Customer B's size fits the Anthropic lease, which is my inference.
- **More contracted.** A $6.7B, six-month cloud deal signed in early July ramps from October [19]. The release counts $14.1B of contracted cloud services sales [2].
- **Capacity.** Nameplate compute was 1.4GW at Jun 30, from 0.4GW a year earlier [2]; management targets more than 2GW by year-end, and Musk said "closer to 10" than 5GW by end-2027 [19].
- **Cursor.** The $60B all-stock purchase, first struck as an option in April [10], closed Aug 14 for 391.0M Class A shares plus 73.5M assumed awards (calc) [3].

